Catastrophic forgetting is invisible. Until now.

When you fine-tune a model on a new task, it silently destroys what it learned before. You don't notice until you test — and by then it's too late.

NeuroFade hooks into your training loop and renders the forgetting process as a live heatmap. Green neurons are alive. Red neurons are forgetting. Dark neurons are dead.

How It Works

Capture Baseline

Run your trained model on the reference task. NeuroFade records per-neuron activation magnitudes as the "healthy" state.

Watch Training

Train on your new task inside viz.watch(). Forward hooks capture activations every N steps and compare against baseline.

See Forgetting

Export as GIF, print a summary table, or integrate into your monitoring dashboard. Layer 7 at 0%? Now you know where to fix.

from neurofade import ForgettingVisualizer

# 1. After training on Task 1
viz = ForgettingVisualizer(model)
viz.set_baseline(task1_loader)

# 2. Train on Task 2 — forgetting happens here
with viz.watch():
    trainer.fit(task2_loader)

# 3. See the damage
viz.summary()         # terminal table
viz.export("forgetting.gif")  # shareable artifact
